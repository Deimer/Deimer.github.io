## About Teravision Technologies

**Teravision Technologies** is a nearshore software development agency headquartered in Latin America with offices across Colombia and Venezuela[cite: 1]. They specialize in building custom web, mobile, and IoT enterprise applications for tech startups and enterprise corporations in North America.

## Technical Scope & Business Impact

At Teravision Technologies, I served as a Mid-Senior (SSR) Android Developer focused on native mobile application development involving complex external hardware communications and high-security protocols[cite: 1].

* **Hardware & Bluetooth Integration:** Integrated mobile applications with external hardware devices via **Bluetooth APIs**[cite: 1], establishing stable data transmission channels for real-time telemetry and hardware control.
* **Low-Latency Serialization with Protobuf:** Implemented **Google Protocol Buffers (Protobuf)** over binary protocols[cite: 1] as an alternative to traditional JSON, drastically reducing payload sizes and speeding up communication response times with external hardware and backend servers[cite: 1].
* **Asynchronous Data Pipelines:** Managed reactive events and asynchronous data streaming from hardware peripherals using **RxAndroid** and **RxJava**[cite: 1], preventing UI thread blocking during continuous data reads.
* **Dependency Injection & Architecture:** Utilized **Dagger 2**[cite: 1] for compile-time dependency injection, guaranteeing modularization and effortless unit test coverage for background services.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partnered with external engineering teams and embedded hardware specialists[cite: 1] to define communication schemas, troubleshoot hardware-software interface edge cases, and maintain strict protocol compliance.
